## 8. Awwwards-style concept: "Del caos a la integración"

### The idea
The name *Integria* suggests **integration** and **integrity**. The site tells that story visually: scattered data points (the client's disconnected systems, data and processes) come together into one connected, glowing blue structure as the visitor scrolls. That is exactly what an IT consultancy does.

The 3D scene is one particle network built in code (no heavy 3D models), which keeps the site fast.

### Scroll story (home page)

| Step | What the visitor sees | Message |
|---|---|---|
| **Preloader** | A counter 0 → 100 while points appear one by one on black | — |
| **Hero** | Thousands of blue and cyan particles drift in darkness and react to the cursor. The headline appears letter by letter. | *"Integramos tecnología, datos y personas."* |
| **01 Diagnóstico** | On scroll, the particles slow down and group into clusters | Understanding the client's current situation |
| **02 Diseño** | Thin lines connect the clusters into a network, like a blueprint | IT strategy, governance and architecture |
| **03 Implementación** | The network locks into a solid 3D structure (a sphere or lattice) that glows blue | Software, data, AI and project delivery |
| **04 Acompañamiento** | The structure rotates slowly and pulses steadily | Support, service management and continuous improvement |
| **Transition** | The screen sweeps from black to white | Moving from complexity to clarity |
| **Services** | Cards in a horizontal scroll; on hover, the card image ripples with a light shader effect | Consulting first, then implementation |
| **Numbers** | Large animated counters: *+30 años*, *PMP · ISO 27001 · Scrum*, *Sector público y privado* | Credibility |
| **Founders** | Two portraits that shift from black-and-white to color on hover, with short bios | *"Tecnología con visión de negocio"* |
| **Final call** | Back to black; the network forms again around the button | *"Conversemos sobre tu proyecto" / "Let's talk about your project"* |

### Signature interactions
- **Smooth scrolling** for the fluid "Awwwards" feel
- **Custom cursor:** a small blue ring that grows over links and buttons
- **Text reveals:** headlines enter line by line
- **Language toggle (ES / EN):** the text scrambles briefly, like code, then settles into the other language
- **Page transitions:** a blue line sweeps across the screen between pages, without a full reload
- **Magnetic buttons** that gently follow the cursor

### Keep it credible
The audience is companies and public institutions, so the effects must support the message, not hide it:
- Clear text and the contact button are always reachable; the first screen must say what the company does within 3 seconds
- Usability counts for 30% of an Awwwards score; a slow or confusing site loses points
- The inner pages (services, about, contact) stay mostly white and readable, with lighter motion

### Technology

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| Site framework, ES / EN pages | **Astro** (fast, simple, built-in multi-language). Next.js if a CMS or app is planned. |
| 3D particle network | **Three.js** with custom shaders. The WebGPU renderer falls back to WebGL automatically. |
| Scroll animation | **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** |
| Smooth scroll | **Lenis** |
| Text effects | **GSAP SplitText** |
| Page transitions | **View Transitions API** |
| Hosting | **Vercel** or **Netlify** (free tier is enough to start) |

All animation runs from a single timing loop so scroll, motion and 3D stay in sync (see `research-claude-skills-and-webgl.md`, Part 3).

### Performance and accessibility
- The 3D scene is generated in code, so there are no large files to download
- Fewer particles on phones; a still image instead of 3D on very weak devices
- Visitors who turn off motion in their system settings get a static version
- All text is real HTML under the 3D canvas, readable by screen readers and Google
- Target: page usable in under 2 seconds on 4G
